We would like to express our deepest gratitude to the “Save a Heart” Association for the support given to us during an extremely difficult period. The help we received not only provided the financial assistance we desperately needed but also gave us the reassurance that we are not alone in our fight 🥹. Our children, Antonia and David, suffer from a rare mitochondrial disease caused by the SUCLG1 genetic mutation, accompanied by profound bilateral hearing loss, which severely affects all their bodily functions. They require daily special treatment, recovery therapies, and permanent care for absolutely all their needs – from drinking water, eating pureed food, to being changed and so much more 🥹 – while we live each day under the constant fear and stress of coping with all these challenges. Your involvement, especially when we traveled with them to Canada for a medical study, was of immeasurable help. Your gesture brought light and hope into our lives! When people unite for a cause, miracles become reality 🙏🏻. We sincerely thank you for your commitment, seriousness, and understanding! A mother with two severely affected children

Before the surgery, Andreea’s life was full of pain and limitations. It was hard for us to see her struggle, tire easily, and become increasingly sad and withdrawn. Scoliosis affected not only her spine, but also her self-confidence. We lived with heavy hearts and the fear that time was working against us. 💬 When we found out that she urgently needed surgery, the costs overwhelmed us. We didn’t know which way to turn. But then something happened that we will never forget: kind-hearted people—both those we knew and complete strangers—started to support us. 💬 We are deeply grateful to the “Save a Heart” Association, which made this life-saving intervention possible. Their support was essential. We thank all the donors—whether they gave a small or large amount, every gesture mattered. We are profoundly thankful to our relatives, Andreea’s teachers, her schoolmates, and close friends who stood by us during the hardest times. 💬 With special gratitude, we thank Dr. Engin Çetin, who performed the surgery with professionalism and care, and the translators from “Liv Hospital” – for their support, their patience, and for making us feel understood in a foreign country and during deeply emotional moments. 💬 The surgery was a success, and today Andreea walks with a straight back and a smile on her face. We’re happy to see her dreaming again and becoming the cheerful little girl she once was. This experience has transformed us and taught us how much good people can do when they come together for a noble cause. 💬 We feel immense gratitude. Thank you to everyone who was part of Andreea’s healing. Our story is about hope, courage, and love – and about the fact that miracles are possible when you’re not alone.

We thank them wholeheartedly for listening to us and standing by our side. Today, thanks to Mr. Vlad Plăcintă and “Save a Heart,” Cosmina is a grown young lady and has been given the chance for a new life!
