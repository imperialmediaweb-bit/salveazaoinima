    About us

    SAVE A HEART ASSOCIATION

    Vision:
    We see a future where every heart has a chance at a full life, regardless of the obstacles presented by illness or material deprivation. We aim to strengthen a community of solidarity, where generosity and empathy transform the destiny of those in suffering.

    Mission:
    Through the "Save a Heart" Association, we are committed to providing medical support to those facing serious conditions, connecting need with generosity. We facilitate life-changing medical interventions and procedures, and encourage solidarity and civic responsibility through donations and community involvement.

    Values:

    Altruism - We put the good of others first, making a difference through every gesture of generosity.
    Solidarity - We believe in the power of community to do good, together being stronger and having a greater impact.
    Integrity – All resources raised are invested with transparency and accountability in causes that need help.
    Compassion – We understand the suffering and hope of each person we come into contact with and dedicate ourselves to relieving their pain.
    Change – We believe that every life saved is a new chance to build a brighter future with endless potential.
    Whether you are a person who wants to give or one who needs help, the "Save a Heart" Association is the bridge between generous hearts and those who aspire to a new beginning. Every donation, whether it's time, energy, or financial resources, contributes to the realization of our vision. Together we can build a world where every heart beats stronger

    Transparency

    For us, transparency is not a slogan, but a basic principle. It means real, accurate and verifiable figures, officially registered on the https://salveazaoinima.ro platform, where each donation is highlighted correctly and in plain sight.

    Transparency does not mean simple posts or written updates on social networks, without meeting donors with concrete evidence. It means open access to clear information: who donated, when they donated and how much they donated, without interpretations or ambiguities.

    We strongly believe that respect begins with honesty. Respect for the child we support, whose life depends on these funds, but especially respect for each donor who has chosen to offer their time, trust and resources for this cause.

    Every euro donated is tracked, reported and justified. We take responsibility for communicating openly and fairly, because only in this way can we build real trust and lasting support.

    This is transparency as we understand it: clear, documented and respectful of everyone involved.

     

    The spiritual protector of the organization is "Saint Hierarch Nectarios of Aegina", the miracle worker.
